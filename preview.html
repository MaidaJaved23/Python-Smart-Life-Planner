Two modules

Pakistan Trip Planner

Costs a trip by travellers, rooms, days and tier, adds a 5% emergency buffer and classifies it as within, exactly within or over budget. Over budget? The optimizer offers only options it re-costed as affordable. Cancelling a modification keeps the original plan.

Monthly Budget Manager

Home and Kitchen or Personal Finance templates with custom categories. Records expenses, flags category and overall overspending, reports the net savings rate and exports a PDF once you confirm.

Rooms         = ceil(Travellers / Room capacity)
Accommodation = Base room x Tier x Rooms x Nights   (0 when Nights = 0)
Total         = (Transport + Accommodation + Food + Activities) x 1.05
Savings rate  = (Available - Actual) / Available x 100%
